# Overview
We had another amazing GodotCon in 2026, with more than 300 people attending 46 talks, 7 workshops, and 13 showcase exhibits. This was our first year having a student showcase as well, thank you to the Brown-RISD Gamedev Club for kicking this off!

Below, you can find and all of the recorded sessions from our YouTube. You can visit the official event site [here]({{ page.event_link }}).
